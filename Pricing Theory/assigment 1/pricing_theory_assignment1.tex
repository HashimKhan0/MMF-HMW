\section*{Question 1}

\subsection*{(a)}

We need to buy stock for \$$K$ at time $T$. To replicate we can:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Borrow $\dfrac{K}{(1+r)^{\tau}}$, \quad $\tau = T - t$
    \item Buy stock, hold till forward expiry
\end{enumerate}

$\rightarrow$ This portfolio is worth
\[
\Pi_T = S_T - \frac{K}{(1+r)^{\tau}}\,(1+r)^{\tau} = S_T - K
\]
\[
V_T = S_T - K \quad \text{(forward contract at time } T\text{)}
\]

Both our portfolio and the forward have the same value at maturity.

$\therefore$ General expression for forward:
\[
\boxed{V_t = S_t - \frac{K}{(1+r)^{\tau}}}
\]

\subsection*{(b)}

\[
V_t = S_t - \frac{K}{(1+r)^{\tau}} = 55 - \frac{52}{(1.05)^{0.75}} = \$4.87
\]

\subsection*{(c)}

\textbf{Sell forward for \$6}

\midrule

To arb the mispriced forward we use two instruments:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Forward $V_t$
    \item Replicating portfolio $\Pi_t$
\end{enumerate}
We buy low \& sell high, noting that the replicating portfolio always equals the no arb value of the forward.

\section*{Question 2}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c}
\toprule
State & 0 & $u$ & $d$ \\
\midrule
Stock (\$) & 60 & 75 & 48 \\
Put (\$) & ? & 0 & 12 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\qquad
$K = 60, \quad \text{payoff} = (60 - S_T)^+$
\end{center}

\subsection*{(a)}

Let $B$ be the amount of bonds we have. Let $N$ be defined similarly for the stock.
\[
\begin{cases}
75N + 1.04B = 0 \\
48N + 1.04B = 12
\end{cases}
\]
which gives
\[
27N = -12 \;\Rightarrow\; N = -\frac{12}{27} = -\frac{4}{9}
\]
Thus
\[
1.04B = -75\left(-\frac{4}{9}\right) \;\Rightarrow\; B = 32.05
\]
\[
V_0 = 60\left(-\frac{4}{9}\right) + 32.05 = \$5.38
\]

\subsection*{(b)}

\[
1.04\,q_u + 1.04\,q_d = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; q_u + q_d = \frac{1}{1.04} = \frac{25}{26}
\]
\[
75\,q_u + 48\,q_d = 60 \;\Rightarrow\; 75\,q_u + 48\left(\frac{25}{26} - q_u\right) = 60
\]
\[
27\,q_u = \frac{180}{13} \;\Rightarrow\; q_u = \frac{20}{39}, \qquad q_d = \frac{35}{78}
\]

\subsection*{(c)}

\[
V_0 = \sum_i V_i\, q_i = 0\left(\frac{20}{39}\right) + 12\left(\frac{35}{78}\right) = \$5.38
\]

The feature via FTAP that admits no arb is the fact that the state price vector $q$ is strictly positive.
